Privacy Policy for Mental Calculation Tricks

Application: Mental Calculation Tricks (Adaptive Coach)

Developer: Pattan Riyaz

Location: India

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Core Privacy Principle: Practice works offline. Your practice sessions, answers, scores, and streak data stay strictly on your device. We do not operate user accounts, and we never upload, store, or sell your personal practice data. Ads, when you are online, are provided by Google AdMob.

1. About Us & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Mental Calculation Tricks ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Pattan Riyaz based in India, handles user data.

Mental Calculation Tricks is an educational training application designed to help learners and competitive exam aspirants master rapid mental calculation techniques, arithmetic shortcuts, and daily speed math habits.

2. On-Device Data Storage (100% Local Practice)

The App is engineered to operate without requiring an internet connection for its core functionality. The following data is generated and stored locally in a private SQLite database and local preferences on your device:

  • Practice Sessions & Answers: Arithmetic topic, question inputs, answers submitted, elapsed time per problem, speed score, accuracy, and date/time stamps.
  • Performance Analytics: Per-topic skill ratings, best times, mistake patterns, and progress statistics.
  • Learning Progress: Completed lesson notebook views and mastery stars.
  • Habits & Streaks: Daily practice minute goals, active day history, and consecutive day streak counts.
  • Preferences: Keypad layout selection, sound effect volume, reminder notification time, and tactile feedback toggles.

No User Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use the App. We do not have access to your local practice records.

3. Android Permissions & Why They Are Used

The App requests only permissions strictly necessary for its features and online advertising:

Permission Type Why It Is Needed
android.permission.INTERNET Network Required by Google Mobile Ads to request banner and interstitial advertisements when the device is connected to the internet. Core practice works fully offline.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Allows the app to check whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data, so ad requests and ad containers collapse cleanly when offline.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Advertising Google Play Services Advertising ID permission used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve ads and prevent fraud.
android.permission.VIBRATE Hardware Provides tactile haptic feedback when tapping the custom number keypad, submitting answers, and toggling interactive buttons.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to deliver optional daily practice reminders at the time you configure in Settings. Reminders are OFF by default.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED System Allows the app's local notification receiver to reschedule your chosen daily reminder after your device restarts or reboots.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We do not collect personal analytics or telemetry ourselves. However, when your device is connected to the internet, third-party advertisements are displayed via Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device in accordance with its own privacy policies. This information may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to infer general location at city or country level)
  • Device identifiers, operating system version, device make and model
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, impressions) and diagnostic crash logs

For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for students, exam aspirants, and general mental math learners. The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

In accordance with Google AdMob policies, the advertising configuration sets the maximum ad content rating to PG (Parental Guidance) and flags the application as not child-directed. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps.

6. Data Retention and Control

Because your practice data and settings are stored locally on your device:

  • Resetting Data: You can permanently erase all sessions, attempts, skill ratings, habit streaks, and coach history at any time inside the App by tapping Settings > Data Management > Reset all data.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the App from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes the local SQLite database and all associated application data.

Because we do not maintain external user accounts or cloud databases, we have no ability to restore or recover your data once deleted.

7. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your information:

  • Local practice data resides within Android's protected application sandbox and is not accessible to other standard applications on your device.
  • All network requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) encryption in transit.

8. Links to Third-Party Websites

The App may contain links to external web pages (such as this Privacy Policy or Google's documentation). If you tap an external link, you will be directed to that third party's website. We strongly advise you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit, as we have no control over their content or practices.

9. Your Legal Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), or other applicable data protection legislation), you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to know what data is collected, withdraw consent for advertising tracking, or request data deletion.

Since we do not store personal practice data on servers, your right of deletion can be exercised directly and immediately at any time by utilizing the "Reset all data" feature in Settings or uninstalling the App.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, legal requirements, or changes to advertising networks. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of the document. We recommend reviewing this page periodically for any changes.
